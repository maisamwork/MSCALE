About PDF Tools

PDF tools remove the friction from working with documents: merge several files into one before emailing them, split a long PDF into single chapters, or compress a scan so it fits an attachment limit. The tools in this category process files directly in your browser using modern PDF libraries, which means your documents are never uploaded to a server.

That local processing is the key difference from most online PDF sites. Contracts, invoices, ID scans and medical documents stay on your device from start to finish. The workflow is deliberately simple — drop a file, choose an action, download the result — and output quality is preserved so text stays selectable and searchable after merging or splitting.

  • Merge multiple PDFs into a single ordered document
  • Split a PDF by page ranges for sharing chapters
  • Compress large scans to meet email attachment limits
  • Keep text selectable — no rasterising or quality loss
  • Process private documents without uploading them anywhere
