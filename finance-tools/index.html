About Finance & Business

Finance calculators turn vague money questions into concrete numbers: what a loan actually costs per month, how much interest compounds over five years, or what a salary works out to after tax. The tools in this category use standard financial formulas and show a clear breakdown, so you can compare options instead of guessing.

The calculators are built for quick decision-making — comparing two loan offers, checking whether a subscription is worth it, or estimating savings growth. Inputs are plain numbers with no sign-up and no data collection, and results include the totals that matter: monthly payment, total interest and overall cost. They are estimates for planning, not financial advice, but they give you a solid number to work from.

  • Calculate loan EMI with interest breakdown
  • Project compound interest on savings over time
  • Work out take-home pay from gross salary
  • Compare costs before accepting a credit offer
  • Plan budgets with clear monthly totals
