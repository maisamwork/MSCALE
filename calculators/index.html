Advanced use of Calculators

1
Chain calculations into workflows

Run one calculator, carry its result into the next: compute a percentage change, feed it into the loan calculator as an extra payment, then check the break-even point of the decision. Each tool keeps its inputs, so a whole analysis takes under a minute.

2
Reverse any calculation

Every formula here works in both directions. Know the payment you can afford but not the loan size, or the final grade but not the exam score needed? Enter what you have and read the missing number straight off the result panel.

3
Sanity-check quoted numbers

Vendors and lenders quote rounded, optimistic figures. Recompute their numbers independently with exact inputs - the difference between their figure and yours is usually a fee or an assumption worth questioning.

About Calculators

Calculators are the most-used category on MSCALE because they answer everyday questions in seconds. Whether you are checking a percentage increase on a price tag, working out a loan repayment, or splitting a restaurant bill, the tools in this section give you an instant answer without ads getting in the way or an account being required. Each calculator has been built with a single job in mind, so the interface stays clean and the result appears the moment you finish typing.

Every calculator here runs entirely inside your browser. The numbers you enter are never transmitted to a server, stored in a database, or shared with third parties, which makes the tools safe to use for salary figures, loan amounts and other personal information. They also work on phones and tablets, so you can run a quick calculation while standing in a shop or reviewing a contract.

  • Check percentage change between two prices before negotiating a deal
  • Estimate monthly EMI payments before speaking to a lender
  • Work out discounts and sale prices during shopping seasons
  • Calculate BMI and simple health metrics in seconds
  • Compute compound interest to compare savings options
