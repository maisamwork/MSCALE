About Text Tools

Text tools solve the small annoyances that interrupt writing every day: a paragraph pasted with inconsistent capitalisation, a list full of duplicate lines, or a document that must hit an exact word count. The tools in this category handle those jobs instantly. Paste your text, click once, and copy the cleaned result back — no formatting is lost and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Writers, students, marketers and developers all rely on these utilities. A content writer can verify that an article meets a 1,000-word requirement, a student can normalise the case of copied notes, and a developer can strip whitespace or sort lines before committing a config file. Because processing happens locally in your browser, even confidential drafts and private notes are safe to paste.

  • Count words, characters, sentences and reading time for essays and articles
  • Convert text between UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case and Sentence case
  • Remove duplicate lines and empty lines from lists and logs
  • Sort lines alphabetically or reverse them
  • Find and replace text across a whole document at once
