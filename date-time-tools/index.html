About Date & Time Tools

Date and time tools settle the questions that cause real mistakes: how many days between two dates, what a Unix timestamp means, what time it is for a colleague in another timezone, or how old someone is in exact years, months and days. The answers update instantly and account for leap years automatically.

These tools are used for deadlines, contracts, travel planning and server logs. A countdown to a project due date, an age calculation for a form, or a timestamp conversion while debugging an API all take seconds. Because the calculations run in your browser with your local timezone respected by default, the results match what your calendar and your systems actually show.

  • Calculate the difference between two dates in days, months and years
  • Convert Unix timestamps to readable dates and back
  • Work out exact age in years, months and days
  • Count down to deadlines, launches and events
  • Check current time across timezones for scheduling
