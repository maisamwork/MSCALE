What Is the Body Fat Calculator (Navy Method)?

Weight alone cannot tell fat from muscle — two people at 80 kg can have completely different body composition. The US Navy tape method estimates body fat percentage from circumference measurements, and it tracks change over time well even where its absolute accuracy is a few percent off. This calculator applies the official formula and places your result in standard fitness categories. How the Body Fat Calculator (Navy Method) Works

The Body Fat Calculator (Navy Method) implements Navy method: %BF = 495 / (1.0324 − 0.19077×log10(waist−neck) + 0.15456×log10(height)) − 450 (men), which is the standard relationship used in textbooks, reference tables and professional practice for this exact question. The page does not improvise a shortcut or a rounded constant — it performs the same operation a careful person would do with a calculator and a formula sheet, just without the slips.

Precision is handled deliberately. JavaScript computes with 64-bit double-precision floating point, so intermediate values keep about 15 significant digits and only the displayed result is rounded. That ordering matters: rounding early is the classic way small errors become large ones, and the tool avoids it by construction.

Because the calculation is local, the tool is also safe for figures you would rather not paste into an unknown website — salaries, invoices, grades, medical numbers. Nothing is transmitted, nothing is logged, and closing the tab clears every field. The result is deterministic too: the same inputs always produce the same output, so you can reproduce any figure you quote later.
